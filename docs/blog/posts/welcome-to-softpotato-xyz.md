# Welcome to the New softpotato.xyz!

Welcome to the new home of **Soft Potato** at `softpotato.xyz`! We have migrated our documentation and learning resources to **Material for MkDocs** to bring everything together under one roof:

<!-- more -->

1. **Soft Potato Documentation & API Reference:** Complete guides and mathematical reference for [`softpotato.constants`][softpotato.constants], [`softpotato.analytical`][softpotato.analytical], and [`softpotato.solver`][softpotato.solver].
2. **Interactive Electrochemistry Examples:** Step-by-step Jupyter notebooks covering techniques like Cottrell chronoamperometry and cyclic voltammetry.
3. **Python & Linux Tutorials:** Beginner-friendly guides to help electrochemists and students get productive with Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, and the Linux command line.
4. **Project News & Blog:** Regular updates on new releases, roadmap milestones, and scientific computing tips.

Stay tuned for upcoming tutorials and Milestone 2 (`v3.2.0`) updates!
